How to use Seclusa Weather

  1. Search or locate — type a city in the search box, or tap the crosshair button to use your current location.
  2. View the forecast — the main card shows today's conditions, sun/moon times, and an arc of daylight. Below it are the hourly, 7 or 14 day forecast, and a wind compass for your area.
  3. Theme — tap Theme for Light/Dark/System. Under Sky background, set separate Day and Night options (orange lines, blue, light blue, teal, green, yellow, orange, red, purple, black, white, or custom). These follow sunrise/sunset at your place — not the Light/Dark setting.
  4. Units — tap °C in the toolbar to change temperature, wind speed, and visibility units.
  5. Conditions — precipitation, wind, humidity, UV, visibility, pressure, air quality, and pollen are grouped on the Conditions card.
  6. Saved on this device — the last place and forecast stay on your device for 3 days after you last used them, then clear themselves. No account. Use Erase my data below to clear sooner.
  7. Offline — with a saved forecast you can still open the app without a connection.
  8. Install — tap the install banner on Android, or on iPhone use Share → "Add to Home Screen".

Deletes the saved city, coordinates, settings, and every cached forecast from this device.
